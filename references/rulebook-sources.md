## Decisions applied in the fixtures

Dream Archaeology: choose a small one-die, declared memory-spend procedure to expose identity cost at the decision point. Retain finite progress and a deadline with explicit failure continuation. Reject importing Fate aspects/points or Blades stress/resistance wholesale: their surrounding economies would enlarge this prototype. The 1d6 thresholds and persistent memory transfer rules are our provisional numerical choices, not an established system or a claim of unprecedented invention.

Courier notes: preserve the supplied diceless constraint; use visible packet capacity and route costs. A random encounter table would contradict the brief, even if another game uses it successfully.

Glass Engine conflict: preserve the synthetic latest decision's fixed four-charge battery. Correct the older formula, overflow example and character sheet as one revision. The generic checker sees only registered dependencies and claims; prose review identifies the contradiction first.

No full text is redistributed. Source gaps remain recorded: these pages do not establish balance for the three examples, nor do they grant a license to unrelated art, settings or complete commercial books.
