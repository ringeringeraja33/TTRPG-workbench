# Rule topics and source references

Use the bundled system procedures for supported rules and the [bibliography](bibliography.md) for attribution. Readers do not need the maintainer's original collection or private indexes.

Find a topic through [coverage](coverage.md), then the relevant system reference. Citations identify author/publisher, work, edition, chapter and page. When relying on the bundled synthesis, preserve its verification scope; do not claim to have reopened the original.

For an uncovered question, consult official public material or a source supplied by the current user. The [optional reading tools](corpus-use.md) support that research. Missing private indexes are not a reason to block supported operations. Keep technical file locations and extraction IDs out of user-facing references.
