# Harbor: GM adaptation dossier

Original 1925 New York harbor office and missing-cargo inquiry. [National Archives: Smugglers, Bootleggers, Scofflaws](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2011/fall/smugglers-bootleggers-scofflaws) discusses Prohibition-era maritime smuggling and Coast Guard records. This supports cargo documentation, vessel movements and official records as plausible context. The missing shipment, employer and characters are original; no real person's guilt is inferred.

Actual comparator: [Doors to Darkness pregenerated investigators](https://www.chaosium.com/content/FreePDFs/CoC/CHA23148%20-%20Doors%20to%20Darkness/Doors%20to%20Darkness%20Pre-Generated%20Characters.pdf), PDF 2–5. The boxer also has academic interests, while scientist and custodian use different technical competencies. Adopt mixed capabilities and connections, not a party of identical specialists. The published sheets supply final values rather than complete allocation ledgers; they cannot establish an original occupation's point budget. Their equipment is not automatically granted to our characters.

| Rule | Baseline → fixture | Reason and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Reporter | Core journalist pattern EDU×4 with eight named skills and CR | Photography is a skill specialty but does not imply owning a camera. Spanish is a specific original contact choice, not a claim about all harbor workers. |
| Cargo clerk | Original eight-skill EDU×4 occupation | Accounting and Law represent manifests/claims; Library Use represents document retrieval. Retains the same 280-point baseline to compare role emphasis. |
| Wealth | CR 20:40 USD cash/1000 USD other assets | Core local PDF 37 supports the 1920 s calculation. Assets are not instant liquid cash. No new large purchase or vehicle. |
| Limits | Cap 80 and no initial Mythos; no interest spending into CR | Declared fixture house constraints. No budget bonus for urban sophistication or for knowing the plot. |
| Access | Credentials and a ledger copy from fictional employer | Scenario permission, not universal access to police or Coast Guard records. Contacts can introduce another route if access fails. |

Both characters spend 280 occupation/150 interest. Distinguish the reporter's interviewing/photography competence from the clerk's accounting/legal reading even where both can use records. A clerk can question staff, a reporter can check deliveries, and both can inspect public-facing evidence. Failed archive access must not block all progress.

Research limits: this inquiry did not establish historical market prices for new equipment or permission rules for every real institution. Any new purchase or legal-access claim needs its own source. The fixtures intentionally use existing goods and a fictional employer to keep these unresolved issues out of the approved initial inventory.
