# 1884 CE, fictional Colorado rail town

Missing freight and outdoor pursuit

Original CoC 7 adaptation. All occupations, cap 80, equipment grants and access assumptions below are approved for this test fixture only. No Pulp HP or bonus pools. These are player-editable proposed characters.

## Creation rules

Use the fixed fixture attributes shown on the cards; actual play must choose an agreed CoC generation method. Age 20–39 EDU improvement is handled separately. Interest pool INT×2; no interest points in wealth or Mythos. Skill cap 80 is a house rule. Untrained specialties start at the profile base. All changed and unchanged bases are shown in the cards; no unknown skill is silently zero.

Allowed roles: Telegrapher, Track inspector. Other concepts can replace a role after the same budget and source audit. No skill is mandated by the mystery.

Currency and ownership: 1920 s core CR 20=40 cash/1000 assets; railway uses these only as an explicit scenario grant, not 1884 price evidence. Starting equipment is existing/loaned as recorded; no historical shopping-price table has been assumed. New purchases require a sourced price or explicit scenario allowance.

Permitted starting equipment: work clothing, travel bag, bread and water, notebook, repair kit. Exclude any unlisted equipment until reviewed. No automatic firearms, cameras, private horses or vehicles.

Team alternatives: talk to the record keeper, examine a physical trail, or use documentary research. Threshold 50 is only a diagnostic of these examples, not a prerequisite to receive clues.
