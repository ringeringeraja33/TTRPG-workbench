# Railway: GM adaptation dossier

Original 1884 Colorado town and freight inquiry. [Library of Congress on standardized railroad time](https://blogs.loc.gov/law/2024/11/whose-time-is-it-anyway-a-brief-history-of-standardized-time-zones-in-the-united-states/) documents the 1883 railway clock change and telegraph synchronization. Inference: timetables and transmitted messages are plausible investigation media in 1884. This does not prove the fictional town's wiring, station layout or every resident's literacy; those are explicit scenario assumptions.

Actual comparator: [Down Darker Trails Keeper Reference Booklet](https://www.chaosium.com/content/FreePDFs/CoC/CHA23151%20-%20Down%20Darker%20Trails/CHA23151%20-%20Down%20Darker%20Trails%20Keeper%20Reference%20Booklet.pdf), PDF 9. Its claim-jumper NPCs combine ranged combat with climbing, social bluffing, listening and concealment. Adopt the idea that frontier participants need more than a gun skill; do not adopt their weapons as mandatory player equipment or their values as creation budgets. The lawful six-page preview does not contain the creation chapter. That limit is recorded rather than filled with an invented official formula.

| Rule | Baseline → fixture | Reason and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Occupations | Core patterns → original Telegrapher EDU×4; Track inspector EDU×2+DEX×2 | Formal record work versus training/manual coordination. Both yield 280 here; they need not yield equal budgets with different attributes. Eight eligible skills plus Credit Rating each. |
| Repair | Retain Electrical/Mechanical Repair bases and budgets | Electrical Repair applies to available telegraph apparatus, not radio, computing or all future electronics. No extra Telegraph skill tax. |
| Information | Library Use retains 20; inspectors emphasize Navigate/Track/repair | Station records and physical route inspection are alternative investigation methods. Reading a record still requires actual access and language. |
| Money | Explicit 40 USD cash and 1000 USD asset **scenario grant** | Uses the familiar core 1920 s scale only as a house grant. It is not an 1884 price conversion or researched wage. Existing equipment costs 0 at creation; no shopping price list is fabricated. |
| Equipment | No horse, firearm, camera or private vehicle automatically granted | Rail travel requires the fictional employer's arrangements; operation or ownership is separately sourced if introduced. No Pulp talent/HP import. |

Telegrapher and inspector each spend 280 occupation/150 interest points; the first covers station records, the second fieldwork. Both can ask questions. The fixed cap 80 is a scenario choice, not a historical skill ceiling. Missing technical schematics can be handled through an established station employee rather than making one exact specialty mandatory.

If a future request requires a gunslinger or full Down Darker Trails build, load the actual occupation, weapon and classic/Pulp creation paragraphs first. The NPC booklet and a product summary do not certify that build. Equipment purchases require period/location price research or an explicit additional allowance.
