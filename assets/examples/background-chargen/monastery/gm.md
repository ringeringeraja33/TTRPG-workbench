# Monastery: GM adaptation dossier

Original c.1000 setting, not a recreation of a published scenario. The library's [England/France 700–1200 project](https://www.bl.uk/about/projects/medieval-england-and-france) provides dated manuscript context. It supports researching manuscript practices; it does not supply a population literacy percentage, wages or a game skill budget. The fictional monastery and inquiry are scenario assumptions. Do not use fifteenth-century scriptorium illustrations as proof of every practice in 1000.

Actual comparator: [Dark Ages third-edition PDF Pack](https://www.chaosium.com/content/FreePDFs/CoC/CHA23165%20-%20Cthulhu%20Dark%20Ages%20-%203rd%20Edition/CHA23165%20-%20Cthulhu%20Dark%20Ages%20-%203rd%20Edition%20-%20PDF%20Pack.pdf), pages 11 and 13, rendered because extraction omitted filled fields. The Guard and Healer distinguish occupational competence: Guard has strong observation/listening and combat; Healer has strong First Aid/Natural World. Both show separate reading/writing and spoken language fields, Status and Library Use base 5. Adopt differentiated capabilities and separate literacy. Do not import their final values or reconstruct their unlisted point allocation. A separately downloaded Pulp sheet is only a cross-check of field names, not this fixture's authority for HP or budgets.

| Rule | Baseline → fixture | Reason and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Profession | Core occupation patterns → original Scribe eight skills, EDU×4; Guide eight skills, EDU×2+STR×2; Status extra | Scribe emphasizes formal study, Guide combines training and physical work. These are explicit house occupations, not quoted Dark Ages formulas. Guide receives 240 rather than 280 at these attributes. |
| Literacy | Spoken Own Language=EDU retained; reading Latin/native language becomes separate base 1 | Avoid making formal textual access automatic. A copied name, a spoken conversation and reading a manuscript are distinct tasks. |
| Archive | Library Use 20 →5 | Existing supplement pattern adopted as a house override; the same expenditure yields 15 fewer points. Oral custodians remain an alternate access route. |
| Wealth | Credit Rating → Status 0; range 10–30;20 occupational points | Status is fictional standing, not a bank balance. A 10-token travel allowance and existing goods avoid inventing a medieval exchange rate. |
| Availability | Remove automobile, electrical repair, firearm and photography entries | These cannot be used as starting technologies in this mundane fixture. Other retained skills describe period practices, not modern capabilities. |
| Shared limits | cap 80, Mythos 0, no interest into Status | Original scenario restrictions; no Pulp resources or combat bonuses. |

Scribe invests 280/280, Guide 240/240; each 150/150 interest. Scribe's archive and Guide's Ride cover different routes. Latin reading 30 is a limited skill, not fluency guaranteed by profession; important text can also be read by an available custodian. An essential lead can come from the delivery trail without entering the archive. No skill names reveal the hidden explanation.

The source pack does not establish this original budget or economic model. If the user requests official Dark Ages rules, mark this profile as incompatible, obtain its occupation paragraphs and rebuild rather than presenting the fixture as official. Preserve this original baseline for comparison.
