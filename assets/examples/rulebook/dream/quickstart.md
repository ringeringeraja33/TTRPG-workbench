# Player quick reference — Dream Archaeology 0.2

You are archaeologists entering four dream archives. Decide which personal memories you can afford to fade. Players own their character's actions and reactions. Use the [full player rules](rules.md) and [two complete cards](characters.md); this sheet is a companion reference.

- Each scene has five shared turns and progress 0–5. Delve, help, recover, exchange or wait each uses one turn. Safe conversation changes no track; risky archive progress requires delve. Agree who acts.
- Delve: name the method, confirm an approach, spend 0–2 owned fresh memories before rolling, then 1d6 + approach + spent cards + pending help. Total 5+: progress +2; 3–4: progress +1 and strain +1; 1–2: progress +0 and strain +2. Cap progress at 5 and strain at 6. Help is consumed. Failed attempts still use a turn and reveal a damaged fragment.
- Help: spend one fresh card for the other's next delve +1 this scene; no stacking, self-help or helping a fractured character.
- Recover: once per character per scene, use one turn to reduce own strain by 2, floor 0. No memory recovery. At strain 6 you can only recover, trade or wait.
- Exchange: with consent, trade one card each. IDs, text and faded flags persist. No duplication or refresh.
- At turn five settle consequences, then determine complete (progress 5) or damaged archive. Both lead onward. Reset progress, help and recovery allowances, retaining strain and cards.
- After four scenes end the expedition: 3–4 archives coherent, 2 contested, 0–1 fragments. If at least two are complete, raise one approach below 2 by 1 once; no resource refund. An early agreed ending counts only completed archives.
