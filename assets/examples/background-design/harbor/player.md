# A Receipt without a Landing — player materials

1925, a fictional New York harbor office. A missing-cargo complaint threatens a worker's livelihood. Documentary and social noir investigation; no real historical person is accused.

Use the [complete creation rules and two cards](../../background-chargen/harbor/player.md). Occupation and interest allocations are unchanged. These are original CoC7 fixture house profiles, not official supplement builds. Optional scene items below are borrowed when permission is granted; they are not new starting possessions.

## Optional scene equipment

### Ledger copy

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Compare this supplied copy with a consenting clerk's entry. Preserves a recorded quantity for comparison; authenticity and missing context remain questions.

One employer copy; contains no police or Coast Guard access permit. Keep provenance notes; loss leaves the original clerk as an alternative. Alternative: Ask the clerk to explain the entry in front of a witness.

### Inspection request card

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Present a fictional employer's request for a supervised look at an idle crate. Identifies the request; the owner may refuse or offer a public viewing.

One signed request, not a warrant, badge or universal dock pass. Record who grants permission; if refused, use the public-facing label or a witness. Alternative: View an exposed delivery label from the public side.

### Receipt notebook

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Write down a voluntary account and let the speaker check the wording. Separates observed quantities from allegations; no persuasion bonus or binding legal effect.

Three blank statement leaves are a scenario allocation. One leaf per account; if exhausted use an oral statement witnessed by both characters. Alternative: Conduct a witnessed oral interview without a written statement.

You can investigate through testimony and public opportunities when specialist tools are missing. Failed attempts may cost time or evidence quality. Decide your own actions and whether to accept offered favors.
