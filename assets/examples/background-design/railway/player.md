# Two Times on One Receipt — player materials

1884, a fictional Colorado railway town. A freight receipt conflicts with a station timetable. Procedural frontier investigation with outdoor pursuit; firearms and horses are not automatically granted.

Use the [complete creation rules and two cards](../../background-chargen/railway/player.md). Occupation and interest allocations are unchanged. These are original CoC7 fixture house profiles, not official supplement builds. Optional scene items below are borrowed when permission is granted; they are not new starting possessions.

## Optional scene equipment

### Station message copy

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Compare the sender, recipient and time notation with a permitted station log. Shows the recorded message exactly as supplied; no guarantee it is truthful.

One employer-provided copy; access covers this message only. Keep dry; if damaged, request a clerk's recollection at a delay cost. Alternative: Ask the clerk to identify the destination and uncertainty orally.

### Track-inspection sketch

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Mark a public inspection route and compare observed landmarks. Helps communicate locations; does not locate tracks or grant a Navigate bonus.

One fictional employer sketch, not a surveyed map or travel ticket. Update only observed landmarks; replace through the station office. Alternative: Travel with the local inspector on the public path.

### Repair kit

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Inspect an isolated, nonoperating lock or latch with the owner's permission. Enables ordinary repair work under core skill rules; no automatic success or damage bonus.

One loan; does not authorize railway operation, sabotage or work on moving equipment. Return parts after use; a missing part requires a station mechanic. Alternative: Have the authorized mechanic inspect it and report findings.

You can investigate through testimony and public opportunities when specialist tools are missing. Failed attempts may cost time or evidence quality. Decide your own actions and whether to accept offered favors.
