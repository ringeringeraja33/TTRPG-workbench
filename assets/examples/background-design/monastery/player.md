# The Missing Winter Leaf — player materials

Around 1000, at a fictional monastery in southern England. A village disputes its winter grain obligation. Investigate through manuscripts, witnesses and the delivery route; restrained, ambiguous folk horror.

Use the [complete creation rules and two cards](../../background-chargen/monastery/player.md). Occupation and interest allocations are unchanged. These are original CoC7 fixture house profiles, not official supplement builds. Optional scene items below are borrowed when permission is granted; they are not new starting possessions.

## Optional scene equipment

### Writing kit

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Prepare a witnessed handwritten copy after access is granted. Preserves the observed wording for comparison; no bonus die or automatic translation.

One kit; ink and writing surface sufficient for three short copies by explicit allocation. Mark each copy; after three, request supplies from the custodian. Alternative: Ask a literate custodian to read aloud; record oral testimony.

### Measuring cord

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Lay beside visible wheel impressions with the guide. Compare widths at two locations; agreement is supporting evidence, not proof of identity.

One cord, no climbing or load-bearing rating supplied. Dry and return it; if lost, use counted paces with lower precision. Alternative: Compare the route with a local cart driver.

### Host introduction token

Optional equipment supplied at the relevant scene by its fictional custodian; not added to starting cards. Explicit loan or supply allocation, no purchase or historically asserted price.

Present the fictional steward's marked token to request a hearing. Establishes who sent the party; the recipient still decides access.

One loaned token, no universal legal or religious authority. Return at departure; loss can be explained by the steward in person. Alternative: Attend the public petition hour.

You can investigate through testimony and public opportunities when specialist tools are missing. Failed attempts may cost time or evidence quality. Decide your own actions and whether to accept offered favors.
