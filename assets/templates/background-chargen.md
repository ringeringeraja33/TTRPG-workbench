# Background-aware creation specification

## Player document (write as a separate file)

- Rule profile: system, edition, supplements, house-rule approval and revision.
- Public premise: date/place, tone, expected activities, technology/supernatural level.
- Assumptions: what can change and which choices would need recalculation.
- Allowed concepts and exclusions: professions, training, affiliations; exceptions and alternatives.
- Characteristics: method, budget or dice evidence, age modifiers, derived formulas.
- Occupation table: name, skill choices, budget formula, wealth range, source or house-rule ID.
- Skill changes: canonical ID, name/aliases, base, eligible pools, cap, fictional scope.
- Resources: initial HP/MP/SAN or system equivalents, special pools and recovery rules.
- Wealth/equipment: currency, cash/assets, allowances, borrowing, availability and prices; mark scenario grants.
- Languages/literacy, social access and travel: actual character choices, not background stereotypes.
- Party connections: why join, how acquainted, player-editable ties and motivations.
- Team opportunities: alternatives for each expected activity, without clue answers.
- Two worked cards: all required fields, allocation ledgers, untrained defaults, equipment and validation status.
